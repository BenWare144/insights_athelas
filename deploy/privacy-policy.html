Privacy Policy

Athelas Insights Helper · Last updated: July 30, 2026

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data. Everything it does happens locally, inside your own browser.

What the extension does

Athelas Insights Helper adjusts the on-screen spacing/layout and adds optional chart-note helper controls on insights.athelas.com pages. It runs only on those pages. It does not read, record, or send the contents of any chart, patient record, or anything else you view or type.

Data we collect

None. The extension:

  • collects no personal, health, financial, or usage information;
  • makes no network requests and contacts no servers;
  • uses no analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising;
  • requests no browser permissions;
  • stores nothing outside the current page in your browser.

Data we share

None. Because no data is collected, none is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
