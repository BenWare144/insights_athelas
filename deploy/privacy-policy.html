Privacy Policy

Athelas Insights Helper · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any data. It makes no network requests of its own. Everything it does happens locally, inside your own browser. A few settings are remembered on your own computer, and never leave it — see “What is kept on your computer” below.

What the extension does

Athelas Insights Helper adjusts the on-screen spacing/layout and adds optional chart-note helper controls on insights.athelas.com pages. It runs only on those pages. It does not record or send the contents of any chart, patient record, or anything else you view or type.

Data we collect

None. The extension:

  • collects no personal, health, financial, or usage information;
  • makes no network requests and contacts no servers;
  • uses no analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising;
  • requests no browser permissions.

What is kept on your computer

So that it can remember your preferences between visits, the extension saves a small amount of information in your own browser's local storage for insights.athelas.com. None of it is ever sent anywhere, and it is visible only to you, in your own browser profile, on that one site:

  • which of the extension's features you have switched on or off;
  • your professional credential and discipline as shown on the note you are signing (for example “DPT”), so the extension can tailor its suggestions. Your name is not saved;
  • a short, capped list of the chart notes on which the extension has already offered its plan-of-care default, so that it offers it only once per note. This list holds record identifiers used by the site's own web addresses; it holds no patient names or clinical information.

The extension also checks for one optional troubleshooting setting that it never sets itself. It is off unless someone turns it on by hand in the browser's developer tools, and while it is off the extension's diagnostic messages describe what it did without including any chart content.

Clearing your browser's site data for insights.athelas.com erases all of it, and the extension carries on working with its defaults.

Data we share

None. Because no data is collected or transmitted, none is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
